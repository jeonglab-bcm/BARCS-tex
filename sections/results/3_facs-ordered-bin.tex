\subsection{Donor-adjusted BARCS concentrates calls on validated IL2RA
regulators}
\label{sec:facs}

We next tested an ordered phenotype with a donor covariate.  GSE242880
contains an IL2RA protein-abundance screen in primary human T cells, with four
ordered FACS bins for each of three donors, 6,000 guides, and 26 regulators
validated by individual knockout and flow cytometry
\citep{freimer2022il2ra,pimentel2024waterbear}.  We assigned each bin a
prespecified location on a latent normal scale, and BARCS fitted one
marker-abundance slope with donor indicators.  Official MAGeCK-MLE received
the same four-bin design.

Bins from one donor partition the same cell pool and are therefore negatively
dependent, whereas BARCS treats each bin as an independent library.  The 593
non-targeting guides show the consequence: before calibration, 0.138 of them
had $p<0.05$.  We therefore calibrated BARCS with these controls.  Because
\texttt{bb\_calibrate\_controls()} estimates its scale from the control tail,
evaluating the same guides after fitting would be circular, so we used
deterministic five-fold cross-fitting and evaluated each guide with a scale
estimated from the other four folds.  The held-out $p<0.05$ rate was 32/593
(0.054), with fold-specific scales from 1.26 to 1.41.  A seeded permutation of
the fold assignment gave 30/593 (0.051), indicating little sensitivity to the
order of guide identifiers.  The production gene analysis used all controls to
estimate one scale, which changes significance but not effect estimates or
their ranking.

Calibrated BARCS recovered 23 of the 26 validated regulators with 61
discoveries, compared with 17 regulators and 72 discoveries for the matched
MAGeCK-MLE fit (Table~\ref{tab:waterbear}).  Waterbear and MAUDE recovered 24
and 25 regulators, but with 79 and 406 discoveries, respectively.  BARCS thus
had the highest fraction of calls on the validation panel among the four
methods (37.7\%, compared with 6.2--30.4\%), and it ranked the validated
regulators near the top of the screen, with a median rank in the top 1.2\% of
genes.  Waterbear models the dependence among sorted bins explicitly; in BARCS,
negative controls diagnose and correct the resulting miscalibration of the
guide tests, and a joint model of the bins would be a natural extension.
